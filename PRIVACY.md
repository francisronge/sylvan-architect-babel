# Privacy

Babel is run by Francis Ronge. Contact: francisronge@icloud.com.

**Sentences and trees.** Every sentence you submit and the tree generated for it
are kept permanently for research and may be published. They are stored without
your IP address or any other identifier, so they can't be traced back to you, or
found and deleted for you. Don't type personal information.

**Who handles your sentence.** It is sent to OpenAI to generate the tree, under
[OpenAI's API data policy](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).
The site runs on Vercel.

**Limits.** To enforce the daily limit, a one-way hash of your IP address is
kept for up to two days.

**Your Tree Bank** stays in your browser; Babel never receives it.

No accounts, ads or analytics.
